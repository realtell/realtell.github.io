About RealTell

AI can now create photos and videos that look completely real. RealTell helps anyone – readers, journalists, teachers, shoppers and creators – quickly check where an image or video came from.

How it works

Many AI tools, including ChatGPT, DALL-E, Sora, Google Gemini and Adobe Firefly, hide a label inside every file they create, known as Content Credentials (the C2PA standard). Cameras and phones store their own details too. RealTell reads these hidden labels and shows you a clear verdict together with the exact evidence behind it.

Honest by design

We don't guess. Tools that claim to “see” AI in the pixels often label real photos as fake. RealTell only reports what is actually stored in the file, and tells you plainly when the evidence is missing.

Private by design

All checks run inside your own browser. Your files are never uploaded, stored or seen by us.
