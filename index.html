How RealTell detects AI images and videos

🏷️ Content Credentials

ChatGPT, DALL-E, Sora, Google Gemini and Adobe Firefly attach a digitally signed label (the C2PA standard) to the files they create. It records which tool made the file and whether it was generated or edited with AI.

🧾 AI metadata

Many generators write an industry tag (IPTC “Digital Source Type”) that says “created by a trained algorithm”. Stable Diffusion apps like Automatic1111 and ComfyUI often save the full prompt and settings.

📷 Camera evidence

Real photos and videos usually carry camera details – make, model, lens, exposure and capture time. Some new cameras and phones even sign their photos with Content Credentials.

What the results mean

ResultMeaning
AI-generatedThe file contains a label from an AI tool, or AI generation settings. Strong evidence.
AI-editedA real photo or video that was changed with AI (for example generative fill or AI object removal).
Likely AI-generatedAn unsigned AI tag was found. These can be added or removed, so treat it as a strong hint.
Camera original / Likely realCamera data or a camera signature was found and no AI labels. Metadata can be copied or faked, so this is evidence, not proof.
No AI labels foundThe file carries no information either way – common for screenshots and files downloaded from social media.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check if a picture is AI-generated?

Drop the image into the box above. RealTell looks for Content Credentials, AI metadata tags, generation settings and camera information, then shows a verdict with the exact evidence it found.

Can RealTell prove an image is real?

No tool can do that with certainty. A missing AI label can simply mean the label was removed. If an image matters – for news, a dispute or a purchase – combine RealTell with a reverse image search and look closely at details such as hands, text, reflections and shadows.

Why does an image I know is AI show “No AI labels found”?

Most likely the labels were stripped. Screenshots, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and X often remove metadata when you upload or download. Midjourney images also usually lack signed credentials.

Can I check Sora or other AI videos?

Yes. Videos saved directly from Sora and other tools that support Content Credentials carry a label RealTell can read. Videos that were screen-recorded or re-encoded usually lose it.

Are my files uploaded or stored?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your photos and videos never leave your device, which makes RealTell safe for private and sensitive files.

Is RealTell free?

Yes – free, unlimited and no account needed.
