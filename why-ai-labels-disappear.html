Why Do AI Labels Disappear From Images?

You generate an image with ChatGPT, send it to a friend, and suddenly no detector can find the AI label. What happened? Here's how metadata gets lost – and what that means when you're trying to check a file.

Where AI labels are stored

AI labels such as Content Credentials and IPTC tags live in a file's metadata – a section of the file separate from the picture itself. The pixels you see and the label describing them are stored side by side. If the file is rebuilt from the pixels alone, the label is left behind.

The most common ways labels get removed

Screenshots and screen recordings

A screenshot creates a brand-new image from what is on your screen. None of the original file's metadata comes along.

Messaging apps

Many chat apps compress photos and videos to save data, producing new files without the original metadata.

Social media uploads

Most platforms re-encode uploads to standard sizes and formats. Some read Content Credentials to display their own “AI info” label, but the file you download afterwards generally doesn't keep it.

Editing and conversion

Cropping, resizing or converting a file (for example PNG to JPG) in an app that doesn't support Content Credentials can drop them. Online converters and compressors usually remove metadata too.

What this means when you check a file

  • A label found is strong evidence – it survived the journey.
  • No label found is the normal state for files shared online and tells you very little.
  • The closer you get to the original file, the more likely labels are intact. Always ask for the original.

Is removing AI labels allowed?

Removing metadata is a normal part of how many apps work, and there are legitimate privacy reasons for stripping data such as GPS location from your own photos. But deliberately removing AI labels to pass off AI content as real can mislead people, may break a platform's rules, and in some places may conflict with transparency laws. If you share AI-generated content, the honest approach is to say so.

Frequently asked questions

Do screenshots remove AI labels?

Yes. A screenshot is a new image and contains none of the original metadata.

Does WhatsApp remove metadata?

Photos sent normally through many messaging apps are compressed and lose most metadata. Sending as a document may keep it.

Can invisible watermarks survive?

Some watermarks hidden in the pixels, such as Google's SynthID, are designed to survive screenshots and compression, but they need special tools to read.
